# DukaanOS — Shop Owner's Daily Guide (Pilot User Handbook)

Welcome to **DukaanOS**! This handbook is designed for store owners, branch managers, and cashiers to manage daily retail operations effortlessly.

---

## 1. Starting Your Day (Login & Overview)

1. Open your browser and go to your DukaanOS link (or open the installed app on your mobile/tablet/PC).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign In**.
3. Your **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)** shows:
   - **Today's Sales & Revenue (Rs.)**
   - **Today's Realized Profit (Rs.)**
   - **Low-Stock Alerts** (Items running out)
   - **Customer Udhaar Summary** (Total pending credit)

---

## 2. Managing Products & Prices

- **Add New Item**: Go to **Products** (`/dashboard/products`) $\rightarrow$ Click **+ Add Product**.
  - Enter Name, SKU/Barcode, Cost Price (Wholesale), and Selling Price (Retail).
- **Update Stock / Inventory**: Go to **Inventory** (`/dashboard/inventory`) to view remaining stock for all items.

---

## 3. Stock Purchasing (Procurement)

When new inventory arrives from your supplier:
1. Go to **Purchases** (`/dashboard/purchases`) $\rightarrow$ Click **+ New Purchase**.
2. Select the **Supplier**.
3. Scan or pick the items and enter the quantity received and wholesale cost.
4. Enter the amount paid to the supplier $\rightarrow$ Click **Submit**.
5. Your inventory stock will increase instantly!

---

## 4. Selling at the Counter (POS Terminal)

Go to **POS (`/dashboard/pos`)**:
1. **Add Items**: Scan product barcodes with your barcode gun or search by product name.
2. **Select Customer (Optional)**:
   - For walk-in cash customers, leave as **Walk-in Customer**.
   - For regular credit customers, search and select their name.
3. **Payment Modes**:
   - **Cash**: Enter the amount handed over by customer.
   - **Udhaar / Credit**: If the customer will pay later or pays partially, choose **Credit**.
4. Click **Complete Sale** $\rightarrow$ A receipt will print or display on screen!

---

## 5. Customer Udhaar & Debt Recovery

- **Check Who Owes Money**: Go to **Customers** (`/dashboard/customers`) to view all customer balances.
- **Record a Payment**:
  1. Click on the customer's name.
  2. Click **Record Payment**.
  3. Enter the cash amount received $\rightarrow$ Click **Save**.
  4. The customer's balance is updated immediately in the ledger.

---

## 6. Daily Reports & Business Health

- **Daily Sales Report**: Go to **Reports $\rightarrow$ Daily Report** to see your gross sales, profit, cash vs. credit split, and best-selling items today.
- **Business Advisor**: Go to **Advisor (`/dashboard/advisor`)** for automated suggestions on low stock, fast-selling goods, and profit margins.

---

## 7. Staff & Attendance

- Go to **Employees (`/dashboard/employees`)** to log daily staff attendance (`Present`, `Absent`, `Late`) and record monthly salary disbursements.

---

## 8. Offline Mode & Internet Disconnection

- If your internet connection drops during peak store hours:
  - DukaanOS will automatically switch to **Offline Mode**.
  - Continue making POS sales as normal — sales are stored securely on your device.
  - When internet reconnects, click **Sync** or let DukaanOS automatically synchronize your offline sales to the server.
